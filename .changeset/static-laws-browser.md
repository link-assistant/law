Add the static laws browser: a Links Notation registry (`data/registry.lino`) with a legal basis for every source in US, EU, CN, IN, RU and GE, one isomorphic converter to a unified law model used in CI, locally and in the browser, a `law` CLI (`validate`, `list`, `fetch`, `probe`, `build`), a static GitHub Pages site that mirrors laws where copying is allowed and loads the rest live in the browser, and a scheduled Laws workflow that refreshes the site and `data/catalog.lino`.
